## Users

**Primary: businesses evaluating a technology partner.** Owners, operators, and managers of small and mid-sized organizations — largely in and around Mar del Plata — who have a manual, repetitive, or software-shaped problem and are deciding who to hire for it. They arrive to judge whether this company is real and capable, and they leave by asking for a quote.

**Secondary, each with a genuine path of their own:**
- **Lingua Campus end users** — people looking for language learning.
- **Paseos con Peques end users** — families looking for outings with young children.

The two secondary audiences are not an afterthought: each business unit gets its own real section on the site, serving its own visitors, not just a portfolio thumbnail. But when the three audiences compete for a decision, the business visitor wins.

## Product Purpose

A brand and portfolio site for **Origen MdP — Costa Tech**, an IT company in Mar del Plata, Argentina.

It presents three services:
- **Desarrollo a medida** — custom software development
- **Consultoría de automatización con IA** — AI automation consulting
- **Capacitación** — training

…and two business units the company owns and operates: **Lingua Campus** and **Paseos con Peques**.

Success is a visitor making contact to request a quote for a service, or discovering and entering one of the company's own products.

## Positioning

Four claims, all confirmed as true, that a neighboring dev shop could not truthfully copy:

1. **We build and run our own products.** Lingua Campus and Paseos con Peques are operating businesses, not demos or case studies. They are proof the development + automation + training offer works end to end, funded on the company's own money and risk.
2. **AI automation is the through-line.** Every service — build, consult, train — aims at automating the client's actual operation. Software is the means, not the product.
3. **Training makes it stick.** Delivery is not complete when a system that the client cannot run is handed over. Capacitación is part of the work, not an upsell.
4. **Local and present.** Mar del Plata — reachable, accountable, in the same city — against remote agencies and offshore shops.

## Operating Context

The visitor is evaluating, not buying on the spot. A quote request is a conversation, not a transaction: the site's job ends at a qualified first contact.

Conversion runs through two confirmed channels, both of which the site must point at:
- **WhatsApp** — direct chat, low friction, the dominant business-contact channel in Argentina.
- **An on-site form that emails the company** — for visitors who want to state the problem before speaking to anyone.

The two business units are live on the web today and have their own end users arriving independently of the B2B pitch.

## Product Principles

1. **Proof before pitch.** The two operating units are the argument. A claim that cannot point at something real does not ship.
2. **Every engagement ends in the client's hands.** Automation and delivery are judged by whether the client can run the result without us. Training is part of the work, not a service tier.
3. **One company, three doors.** A business, a language learner, and a family must each find their path without the other two getting in the way — and without the site fragmenting into three sites.
4. **Nothing fabricated.** Where proof is absent, the gap is designed around honestly. No placeholder ever reads as a real client, number, or endorsement.
5. **Local is a fact, not a slogan.** Mar del Plata presence shows up as reachability and accountability — in how contact works — rather than as a line of copy about being local.
